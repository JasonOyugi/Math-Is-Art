# Revised Math Is Art images

- 05_Lemniscate: The drawn oval is the real locus of the smooth genus-one quartic v² = 1 − u⁴. The point (0,1) supplies a rational base point. The former infinity-symbol motif was not a faithful plot of this curve.
- 08_Riemann_Roch: A blue torus denotes genus one, without the earlier dotted linking arrows. The displayed formula is Riemann–Roch for a divisor D on a smooth projective curve.
- 09_Elliptic_family: Every blue line is sampled from y² = x³ − x + b for b = −1/4, 0, 1/4. Its discriminant 16(4 − 27b²) is nonzero for all three choices.
- 13_Group_law: An adaptation of the supplied hand-drawn construction. On E: y² = x³ − x + 1, P = (0,1), Q = (1,1), and the horizontal chord y = 1 also meets E at (−1,1). Reflecting gives P + Q = (−1,−1).

The four SVGs are editable sources. The PNGs are 1500 × 1500 poster exports. All other numbered PNGs are unchanged from the supplied archive.
